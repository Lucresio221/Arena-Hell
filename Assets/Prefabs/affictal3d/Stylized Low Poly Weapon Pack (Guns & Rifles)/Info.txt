This pack includes a collection of stylized low-poly weapon models designed for game development. The assets feature simple geometry and colorful designs, making them ideal for stylized, arcade, and low-poly game environments.

All models are optimized for performance and are suitable for mobile, indie, and PC projects. The weapons can be easily integrated into FPS, TPS, survival, and action games.

The pack is designed for quick implementation, allowing developers to use the assets directly or customize them to fit their project style.
